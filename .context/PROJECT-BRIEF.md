# PROJECT BRIEF — GHOST PORTFOLIO 2.0

## 1. Mission
Architect and build **GHOST-PORTFOLIO-2.0**, an executive digital portfolio ecosystem for **Danilo Novais** that communicates **Strategic Thinking**, **Creative Leadership**, **Visual Excellence**, and **Technological Innovation (Creative Tech & Generative AI)**.

## 2. Professional Positioning
- Senior Creative Director & Head of Creation
- Creative Strategist & Brand Architect
- Art Director & Motion Director
- Creative Technologist & Generative AI Specialist
- Creative Operations & Design Systems Leader

## 3. Target Deliverables
- **Phase A — Intelligence & Architecture:** Discovery report, product & content strategy, Ghost Design System 2.0 specification, technical & database architecture, 19-agent ecosystem, 17 modular skills, and PREVC workflows.
- **Phase B — Creative Experience:** Interactive prototypes for Home, Portfolio, and Modular Case Studies validated against Ghost Design System 2.0.
- **Phase C — Engineering & Launch:** Clean Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4 + R3F/Motion implementation, content migration of 15 cases, 15 quality gates verification, and staged deployment.
